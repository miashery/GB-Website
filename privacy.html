Gizlilik ve veri ilkeleriPrivacy and Data Principles

Giggles & Bloom ailelerin ve cocuklarin verisini dikkatle, amacla sinirli ve guven odakli sekilde ele alir. Giggles & Bloom treats family and child data with care, purpose limitation, and trust at the centre.

Veriniz size aittirYour data belongs to you

Rezervasyon, uyelik, cocuk profili, ruh hali notlari ve iletisim bilgileri yalnizca hizmet sunmak, guvenligi saglamak ve aile deneyimini desteklemek icin kullanilir. Booking, membership, child profile, mood notes, and contact data are used only to deliver services, protect safety, and support the family experience.

Temel ilkelerCore Principles

Amacla sinirliPurpose limited

Veri, belirli bir hizmet veya guvenlik ihtiyaci icin toplanir.Data is collected for a clear service or safety need.

Minimum veriMinimum data

Ihtiyac olmayan bilgileri istememek temel tasarim ilkesidir.Avoiding unnecessary information is a product principle.

Ozel notlar ozel kalirPrivate notes stay private

Ruh hali gibi hassas notlar, paylasim izni olmadikca aile veya ekip icinde acik edilmez.Sensitive notes, such as mood reflections, are not shared with family or staff unless permission is given.
